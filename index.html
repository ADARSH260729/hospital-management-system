Hospital Architecture

Hospital architecture focuses on designing functional, efficient, and patient-centered healthcare facilities. Key considerations include optimal space utilization, natural lighting, ventilation, accessibility, and infection control. It emphasizes creating healing environments with modern technologies, ergonomic layouts, and sustainable materials
